How I Work

Engineering Principles

Identity Is the Control Plane

Every access decision starts with identity. Before writing detection rules or hardening infrastructure, I make sure authentication, authorization, and the identity lifecycle are correct.

Least Privilege Is a Process

Least privilege is not a one-time configuration. It requires reviewing actual access, comparing it against what is required, and removing what is not. I design for periodic access reviews and automated permission analysis.

Automate the Lifecycle

Manual access provisioning creates drift and delay. I build automation that connects source-of-truth systems to directories and cloud platforms, with dry-run modes, audit logs, and idempotent operations.

Think Like an Attacker, Build Like a Defender

My offensive-security background taught me how identity systems fail: token replay, group membership abuse, MFA fatigue, and permission drift. I use that knowledge to build identity controls that account for real attack paths.
